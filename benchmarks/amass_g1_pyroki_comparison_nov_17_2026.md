# AMASS on the Unitree G1: FRDV 2.0 and a ProtoMotions/PyRoki retarget

FRDV 2.0 under several settings is compared with **the baseline**: the AMASS-to-G1 retarget made with the ProtoMotions pipeline (PyRoki solver), as its solver writes it. The data are the 15,648 AMASS clips present in both retargets (496 subjects in 22 AMASS sub-datasets; a subject is a sub-dataset/subject folder). Every condition is read through the MuJoCo Menagerie Unitree G1 model, one revision for all conditions. Four aspects are read: fidelity to the human motion (limb direction error, end-effector error and pelvis heading error), ground contact, self-contact on the robot's link meshes, and tracking by policies trained with BlinkMimic2 (github.com/jyl681/blinkmimic2; "the tracker" below), with SuperMimic (github.com/HybridRobotics/supermimic) as a second tracker on three motion clusters. FRDV default against the baseline was declared the main kinematic comparison; the other FRDV settings are additional conditions. The readings and decision rules were written down before the corresponding results were opened; some rules for combining seeds were written after those results had been computed and before they were opened. The link-mesh self-contact reading, constrained FRDV and the tracking evaluation were designed after the other kinematic results were known.

In the kinematic comparison, brackets give the 95% confidence interval (95% CI) of FRDV minus the baseline, from a bootstrap over subjects; in tracking they are defined under Tracking evaluation. "Points" are percentage points. **Fitted links** are the links whose positions a retarget fits to the human motion; **neighbouring links** are links next to them that neither method fits, except ankle-roll, on which both methods fit a point (the baseline the link, FRDV a toe point offset from it). A **reference motion** is the retargeted motion a tracking policy is trained to follow, and **executed** means performed by the simulated robot under the policy. **Tracking loss** is the executed reading minus the same reading on the reference motion. The **main tracking comparison** is auto scaling + clip grounding against the grounded baseline. A tracking difference is **established** or **separated** when it passes the decision rule of its part of the tracking evaluation. A statement marked **from one independent reading** was computed once, outside the decision rules, and not repeated. Conditions are defined under Data and conditions, readings under What each reading means.

## Summary

- **FRDV default against the baseline.** Limb direction error differs in sign between the link sets, −0.40° [−0.67, −0.13] on fitted links and +0.69° [+0.44, +0.98] on neighbouring links, so no limb direction difference is stated. End-effector error (+0.014 [+0.009, +0.019]) and pelvis heading error (+1.98° [+1.43, +2.78]) are higher for default.
- **Ground contact.** The baseline's solver output has deep floor penetration (lowest collision point more than 3 cm below the floor) in 5.76% of clips, and in 53.5% of the 475 floor-level clips (human pelvis below 0.5 m in at least 10% of frames); its pipeline's per-frame grounding removes it. Every FRDV condition has 0%, by construction, because FRDV keeps the robot's collision geometry above the floor as a solver constraint. Stance height spread is 0.75 cm for the baseline and 0.07 cm for default.
- **Auto scaling + clip grounding**, one of four FRDV conditions without constraints, singled out after all four were read (a selection): limb direction error −4.32° [−4.40, −4.24] on fitted and −1.29° [−1.38, −1.21] on neighbouring links, end-effector error −0.070 [−0.072, −0.069]. Self-contact deeper than 1 cm is 43.0% of frames against 11.3% (+31.8 points [+29.2, +34.3]), and foot skate is +0.012 m/s [+0.011, +0.014]. Auto scaling alone is as high in self-contact (40.6%); the two conditions with the hand-set scale (10.4% and 11.6%) are not distinguished from the baseline (95% CIs include zero).
- **Constrained FRDV** keeps most of the limb direction difference (−4.25° [−4.34, −4.16] fitted, −1.20° [−1.29, −1.12] neighbouring). Its share of frames deeper than 2 cm is below the baseline's (2.2% against 4.7%), but at 1 cm it stays above (21.8%; +10.6 points [+8.6, +12.5]), beyond the target written down for it (within 2 points of the baseline's). Its pelvis heading error is higher than the baseline's (+1.41° [+1.17, +1.69]), and its per-frame solver is about 42 times slower than FRDV's whole-clip solver. No FRDV condition here combines lower limb direction error on both link sets with self-contact at or below the baseline's at 1 cm.
- **Smoothness.** Joint jerk (99th percentile per clip, median over clips) is about 6–13 times the baseline's in the FRDV conditions (1,221–2,476 against 190 rad/s³; default 1,691). FRDV follows the human motion's jerk (knee: 1.20 times the human's for default), and the baseline is smoother than the human (0.14 times). This is descriptive, with no 95% CI.
- **Tracking, people not seen in training.** Policies trained with BlinkMimic2's small-dataset preset on the clips of all but 85 people (5 seeds per condition in the main tracking comparison) are read on 600 clips of 71 of those people. Against the grounded baseline, auto scaling + clip grounding has lower executed limb direction error on fitted links (−3.30° [−3.54, −3.05]) and on neighbouring links (−1.71° [−1.95, −1.44]; the second tracker reverses the neighbouring-link sign in one cluster) and lower end-effector error (−0.053 [−0.056, −0.051]). It has more executed self-contact deeper than 1 cm (+8.2 points [+5.7, +11.1], 1.8 times its seed range). Each of these is established. This is a trade-off and a level difference for one split, on people who largely perform motions seen in training (88% of labelled clips share their sub-dataset and motion label with a training clip). It is not a statement about which retarget generalises better to people not seen in training. It includes the reference-motion difference: on fitted links and end-effector error, most of it is that difference carried through tracking, and FRDV's tracking loss is larger. The self-contact excess is shallow (+0.43 points deeper than 2 cm). BlinkMimic2's library-scale preset gives the same signs (signs only).
- **Completion.** On people not seen in training, FRDV conditions complete fewer trials. Auto scaling + clip grounding completes 0.959 against 0.998; this is not established, because in one sub-dataset group the difference is exactly zero. Default's deficit (0.950) is established, and no fidelity difference is established for default. On the per-cluster policies, both conditions of the main tracking comparison complete 1.000.
- **Whole clip set, trained clips.** One policy per condition and seed is trained on all clips and read on its training clips. No comparison involving FRDV is established there: one training of each FRDV condition ended in a segmentation fault, and under a rule written down after three of the four faults were found and before any reading of the whole-clip-set or split policies was opened, a seed without a final policy prevents an established result (see Segmentation faults). Descriptively, and not as a result, the seeds with a final policy give −3.29° on fitted links and +7.4 points of executed self-contact for the main tracking comparison.
- **Per cluster**, on three rule-selected clusters read on training clips with 3 seeds (seed-range rule, no 95% CI): the main tracking comparison is separated in every cluster, with lower limb direction error on both link sets and lower end-effector error for FRDV, and more executed self-contact. Constrained FRDV, whose reference motions have about half the self-contact of auto scaling + clip grounding, has more executed self-contact than it at 1 cm on the link meshes (separated), but less on the tracker's collision capsules and at 3 cm; the cause is not established. It completes 0.842 in the floor-level cluster.
- **Second tracker** (SuperMimic, one seed per cluster, training clips, signs only): the same sign in all three clusters for limb direction on fitted links, end-effector error and executed self-contact. On neighbouring links one cluster reverses.
- **Coverage.** FRDV does not load SMPL-X files (2,301 clips) or files with more than 16 shape coefficients (MOYO, 388 clips), so the paired set is 85.3% of the baseline's clip list.

## Data and conditions

The baseline's clip list has 18,340 clips. FRDV loads 15,651 of them: it does not load the 2,301 SMPL-X clips (WEIZMANN, CNRS) or the 388 MOYO clips, which carry 300 shape coefficients where the loader reads 16. Three clips of 2–3 frames are too short for the readings, which leaves 15,648 paired clips.

| Condition | What it is | Used in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| baseline | the baseline's solver output with no grounding; in tracking it is called the **ungrounded baseline** | kinematic comparison and tracking |
| grounded baseline | the baseline with its pipeline's per-frame grounding, which shifts each frame vertically so that the lowest robot collision geometry is 2 cm above the floor; the form the pipeline provides for training tracking policies | tracking (main tracking comparison) |
| baseline grounded on the tracker's model | the same rule, with the lowest surface of the tracker's G1 collision model at 0 m | tracking |
| default | `retarget robot=g1 dataset=amass` with no overrides: hand-set factors [0.87, 0.83, 0.75] on the human joint positions along the AMASS x, y and z axes, no ground mode | kinematic comparison and tracking |
| auto scaling | `scaling=auto`: segment lengths taken as medians over each clip and adjusted to the robot's proportions | kinematic comparison |
| clip grounding | default plus `dataset.ground.mode=clip` (hand-set scale): the human motion is shifted so that its lowest ankle over the clip is on the floor | kinematic comparison |
| auto scaling + clip grounding | `scaling=auto dataset.ground.mode=clip` | kinematic comparison and tracking (main tracking comparison) |
| constrained FRDV | auto scaling + clip grounding on a G1 model with added capsules, with 14 self-collision pairs at zero margin (settings under Reproduce) | kinematic comparison and tracking |

Grounding is a vertical shift per frame, so it changes only deep floor penetration and stance height spread; in the kinematic sections "the baseline" is the ungrounded form. Every FRDV condition keeps the robot's collision geometry above the floor as a solver constraint; the ground mode also shifts the human motion. The baseline fits the pelvis contour (the pelvis's outer shell), the hip-pitch, knee, ankle-roll, foot, shoulder-pitch, elbow and wrist-yaw links. FRDV fits the pelvis, hip-pitch, knee, ankle-pitch, ankle-roll, shoulder-pitch, elbow and wrist-yaw links.

Alignment of the two retargets with the human motion:

- Baseline frame k is compared with AMASS frame k·s, where s = round(AMASS frame rate / baseline frame rate). The baseline is at 30 Hz for 10,976 of the 15,651 clips FRDV loads and at 50 Hz for 4,675. FRDV keeps the AMASS frame rate and is read at the same AMASS frames; every derivative is taken on the baseline's frame grid.
- A **heading offset**, one constant rotation per method, turns the human motion into that method's world coordinates: −1.3° for the baseline and 89.1° for every FRDV condition. Each is the circular median of the per-clip heading difference (robot minus human) over a 212-clip calibration subset of the paired clips, leaving out floor-level clips and clips with weak heading agreement. On that subset, baseline forward kinematics through the Menagerie model reproduces the baseline's own link positions to 2e-7 m, after one constant translation per clip.
- Shifting the human motion by −4 to +4 AMASS frames (15,645 clips with at least 10 frames inside the shifted range) gives the lowest median limb direction and end-effector errors at a shift of 0 for the baseline, default, auto scaling and auto scaling + clip grounding. Interpolated between shifts, default's minimum on fitted links lies at −0.46 frame (−0.20 for auto scaling + clip grounding, +0.04 for the baseline); at each clip's own lowest shift, default's paired difference moves by less than 0.02°.
- Under ±1° changes of either heading offset, every limb direction difference of the four unconstrained FRDV conditions keeps its sign and 95% CI, and so do the pelvis heading differences of default and clip grounding. Those of auto scaling (+0.07° [−0.11, +0.26]) and auto scaling + clip grounding (−0.26°; worst case −0.05° [−0.17, +0.06]) do not. This sensitivity was not computed for constrained FRDV.

## What each reading means

| Reading (unit) | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Limb direction error, fitted links** (°) | Mean over frames and 8 segments of the angle between a human bone and the robot segment between matching link origins: shoulder-pitch→elbow, elbow→wrist-yaw, hip-pitch→knee and knee→ankle-pitch, both sides. These are FRDV's fitted links and the baseline's except the ankle end (it fits ankle-roll), so the reading partly repeats each solver's own objective. |
| **Limb direction error, neighbouring links** (°) | The same bones against shoulder-roll→elbow, elbow→wrist-roll, hip-roll→knee and knee→ankle-roll. Neither method fits the shoulder-roll, wrist-roll or hip-roll links; both fit a point on ankle-roll. |
| **End-effector error** (dimensionless) | For hands and feet, the vector from shoulder or hip to the effector in the human's or the robot's own heading coordinates, divided by that body's shoulder-to-hand or hip-to-foot chain length. The error is the norm of the human–robot difference, averaged over the 4 effectors and all frames. |
| **Pelvis heading error** (°) | The absolute difference between the robot's and the human's hip-axis heading after the heading offset, averaged over frames. |
| **Deep floor penetration** (% of clips) | The share of clips whose lowest collision point goes more than 3 cm below the floor in some frame. |
| **Stance height spread** (cm) | Per clip, the standard deviation of the lower robot foot's height over frames where the human foot is in contact. |
| **Foot skate** (m/s) | Per clip, the mean horizontal sole speed while the human's same foot is in contact. |
| **Human foot contact** | A foot is in contact when its lowest vertex is within 4 cm of the floor and moves slower than 0.40 m/s. |
| **Floor-level clip** | The human pelvis is below 0.50 m in at least 10% of frames. |
| **Pelvis twist** (% of clips) | The clip's mean pelvis-to-shoulder twist differs from the human's by more than 20°, or its heading difference after the heading offset exceeds 20°. |
| **Joint jerk** (rad/s³) | The 99th percentile of joint jerk within a clip. |
| **Self-contact** (% of frames) | The share of frames in which some candidate link pair overlaps by more than the stated depth (1 cm unless stated) on the link meshes; on the tracker's motion it is **executed self-contact**. |
| **Completion** (share of trials) | The share of trials that reach the clip end without termination. |

The **link meshes** are the G1 visual meshes, one solid per link. The 404 candidate link pairs are all non-parent-child pairs except the two that overlap in the zero pose. The overlap depth is the deepest point of either link inside the other, from a 1.5 mm distance grid with a winding-number inside test for open meshes. Depth cannot exceed the thinner link's inradius (the deepest readings all stop at 4.84 cm), so readings above 3 cm underestimate the overlap.

Kinematic differences are paired per clip: the median of per-clip differences, or the mean for shares, with the 95% CI defined above.

## Tracking evaluation

The tracking evaluation has four parts, given in this order: policies trained without the clips of 85 people and read on those people's clips; policies trained on the whole clip set and read on their training clips; per-cluster policies; and a second tracker on the per-cluster clips.

- **Training.** The primary preset is BlinkMimic2's small-dataset preset, `PRESET[baseline]` in its `scripts/presets.sh` (BlinkMimic2's name, unrelated to the baseline retarget), used with `--no-pi_ref_skip`, which removes the reference-pose skip connection from the policy's action, and with 4,096 environments. The averaged-weight checkpoint (exponential moving average) after 7,000 iterations is used. BlinkMimic2's library-scale preset (`PRESET[main]`) is used for the main tracking comparison only, and only for its signs.
- **Reference motions.** Every condition is put on the baseline's frame grid and resampled to 50 Hz (linear for positions and joints, slerp for the pelvis orientation). The tracker does not shift the reference motion's height, and it simulates self-collision on its own collision capsules. It keeps the robot's heading, so the executed heading is not re-aligned to the reference motion.
- **Trials.** Each clip gets 5 trials per policy from its first frame, with no noise and the preset's terminations: pelvis height error above 0.25 m, or pelvis tilt error above 0.8 (difference in the vertical component of the pelvis's up axis). Readings use the frames before the trial terminates; the first frame is the reference motion's own.
- **Seeds without a final policy.** A seed without a final policy prevents an established result for every comparison involving its condition. A rerun with the same seed may take its place only if a cause independent of the condition is identified before the results are computed; none was (see Segmentation faults). The rule was written down after three of the four faults had been found and before any reading of the whole-clip-set or split policies was opened.

### People not seen in training

- **Split.** AMASS subject folders are merged into **people** when they share a person name, a recording session, a subject ID across two sub-datasets, or recordings. This gives 454 people, each an identity as far as the metadata tell, not a confirmed person. Within each of the 13 sub-datasets that have at least 5 people, the first 20% (rounded) of its people, ranked by a hash of their alphabetically first subject folder, are left out of training, 85 people in all. People of sub-datasets with fewer than 5 people always stay in training. The split is one draw.
- **Policies.** They are trained on all paired clips of at least 1 s except those of the 85 people. The grounded baseline and auto scaling + clip grounding have 5 seeds each; every other condition has 3.
- **Evaluation clips.** 600 clips of 71 of the 85 people, drawn by hash from their clips of 1–60 s. Clips of the three clusters of the per-cluster part are left out, and so are all clips of five CMU people whose recordings are renamed re-releases of training subjects. 470 of the 532 clips with a motion label (88%) share their sub-dataset and motion label with a training clip; of the other 62, 61 are KIT clips (from one independent reading), and 68 clips have no label. These are therefore people not seen in training who largely perform motions seen in training.
- **Decision rule.** A difference is the mean over seeds of the per-seed clip mean, FRDV (or another baseline form) minus the grounded baseline. It is **established** when (1) it exceeds the larger of the two conditions' seed ranges (the maximum minus the minimum over seeds); (2) its 95% CI excludes zero after Holm's step-down correction over the four readings of that comparison; and it has the same sign (3) in the clip mean and in the mean over sub-datasets with at least 20 clips, (4) for limb direction, on both link sets, (5) on the completed clips and on all 600, and (6) in each of three sub-dataset groups: KIT, BioMotionLab_NTroje, and all others. The Holm correction is within one comparison, not across comparisons or across the parts of the tracking evaluation.
- **95% CI.** A bootstrap over people within those groups. It treats the trained policies and this one split as given, so variation from split to split is not in it. The brackets below are the unadjusted 95% CI; requirement (2) uses the Holm-adjusted level, one reason why default's end-effector difference is not established although its bracket excludes zero. Limb direction, end-effector error and self-contact are read on the **completed clips**, those that every trial of every seed of both conditions reaches the end of: 565 of 600 for auto scaling + clip grounding, 561 for default. For auto scaling + clip grounding, the differences on all 600 clips stay within about 6% of their completed-clip values; default's differ more (fitted links +0.39° on all clips against +0.17°), and none of them is established either way.

- **Completion of auto scaling + clip grounding.** The difference is not established because it is exactly zero in the BioMotionLab_NTroje group (KIT −0.090, others −0.022). It also does not keep its sign when the clips flagged by BlinkMimic2's feasibility screen of the reference motions (raised ground, floor penetration, floating feet) are left out. From one independent reading, the failures concentrate in about 12 to 23 clips that every trial of every seed fails, mostly on stairs and platforms, and they end in a pelvis-height termination. The ungrounded baseline completes 0.953; its difference of −0.045 is not established either.
- **Default.** Its completion deficit is established; from one independent reading, its sign in the BioMotionLab_NTroje group rests on one clip. No fidelity or self-contact difference is established. End-effector error is higher for default (+0.013), but that difference fails the Holm correction and the sub-dataset-group sign.
- **Other conditions.** Neither the ungrounded baseline nor the baseline grounded on the tracker's model is established as different from the grounded baseline on any reading; the nearest case, the tracker's-model form on fitted links (−0.14°), is below its seed range (0.15°). Nothing is established for constrained FRDV, against either the grounded baseline or auto scaling + clip grounding, because one of its trainings ended in a segmentation fault.
- **Robustness.** For auto scaling + clip grounding, limb direction, end-effector error and self-contact keep their sign without the largest person and without CMU people.
- **Library-scale preset** (3 seeds per condition, 564 completed clips). The main tracking comparison has the same signs: completion −0.041; limb direction −2.86° on fitted links and −1.19° on neighbouring links; end-effector error −0.044; self-contact +7.1 points. That is 82–87% of the small-dataset preset's differences on fitted links, end-effector error and self-contact, and 70% on neighbouring links. From one independent reading, this preset mainly reduces the grounded baseline's tracking loss. Its training ran differently for the two conditions: on the whole clip set, FRDV's mean episode length during training was about a third of the grounded baseline's or less (61–70 against 210–249).
- **Change from trained to unseen people.** For each policy, the gap is its reading on these 600 clips minus its reading on a set of trained clips. The policies trained on the whole clip set saw both sets, so subtracting their gap removes the difference in clip difficulty; the comparison is that difference of gaps, FRDV minus grounded baseline. It has no result, because one of the whole-clip-set trainings ended in a segmentation fault. Descriptively, on the seeds with a final policy, it is near zero: completion −0.002 [−0.009, +0.003]; limb direction −0.0004° [−0.072, +0.075] on fitted links and −0.02° [−0.15, +0.10] on neighbouring links; end-effector error +0.0003 [−0.0015, +0.0020]; self-contact +0.19 points [−0.84, +1.25]. This is not a result that the two retargets generalise equally well.

### Whole clip set, trained clips

One policy per condition and seed is trained on all 15,597 paired clips of at least 1 s: 5 seeds for the main tracking comparison and 3 for each other condition. It is read on 600 of its training clips, drawn by hash from clips of 1–60 s, leaving out the 120 per-cluster evaluation clips. The decision rule is the one above, and the 95% CI is a bootstrap over people. Neither other baseline grounding form is established as different from the grounded baseline. No comparison involving FRDV is established, because one training of auto scaling + clip grounding (of five), of default and of constrained FRDV (of three each) ended in a segmentation fault.

Descriptively, and not as a result, the four seeds with a final policy give, for auto scaling + clip grounding minus the grounded baseline: completion −0.020 [−0.034, −0.008]; limb direction −3.29° [−3.49, −3.06] on fitted links and −1.59° [−1.79, −1.37] on neighbouring links; end-effector error −0.052 [−0.054, −0.049]; executed self-contact deeper than 1 cm +7.4 points [+5.1, +10.6]. Levels, grounded baseline → auto scaling + clip grounding: completion 0.998 → 0.978 (all 600 clips); on the 580 completed clips, limb direction error on fitted links 10.92° → 7.63°, end-effector error 0.145 → 0.094, executed self-contact deeper than 1 cm 2.0% → 9.5%. The library-scale preset gives the same signs: completion −0.021, limb direction −2.83° (fitted) and −1.06° (neighbouring), end-effector error −0.044, self-contact +8.3 points.

### Per cluster, training clips

- **Clusters.** Three clusters of an existing 50-cluster partition of AMASS, published with per-cluster tracking policies trained on the baseline. They were chosen by rule among the 40 clusters with at most 400 clips whose published policy was trained on a 48 GB card. The **floor-level cluster** has the highest share of floor-level clips (328 clips). The **self-contact cluster** has the highest self-contact under auto scaling + clip grounding, on a coarser convex-envelope reading (105 clips). The **typical cluster** is closest to the medians of both shares (104 clips).
- **Policies and trials.** There is one policy per condition, cluster and seed (6 conditions × 3 clusters × 3 seeds = 54 trainings), trained on all clips of the cluster on RTX 4090 cards: 24 GB for the floor-level cluster and 48 GB for the other two, so card memory is confounded with the cluster, not with the condition. 10 of the 54 trainings, all with the second seed, were trained on cards with a throttled memory clock; leaving that seed out keeps every separated difference of the main tracking comparison separated. The first 40 clips of each cluster are read; all of them are training clips.

### Segmentation faults

Of the 66 trainings on the two large clip lists (whole clip set and split, both presets, reruns and additional seeds included), 4 of the 36 on FRDV conditions ended in a segmentation fault: default and auto scaling + clip grounding once each on the whole clip set, and constrained FRDV once on each list. None of the 30 on baseline conditions did. The cause is not known. The default training's log ends after the simulator reported an overflow of its constraint capacity (1,699 rows needed); the other three logs give no message. The three faults on the whole clip set fell within about three minutes of each other on two machines. The auto scaling + clip grounding training began to diverge, with losses rising by two orders of magnitude, about 1,000 iterations before its fault and within half a minute of default's fault on the other machine; the other three trainings were normal up to their faults. Both an instability that depends on the condition and an event shared by the machines are consistent with these facts. Each rerun with the same seed reached 7,000 iterations. The counts do not establish that FRDV conditions cause the faults, or that they do not. Under the rule for seeds without a final policy, this leaves without a result every whole-clip-set comparison involving FRDV, both comparisons of constrained FRDV on people not seen in training, and the change from trained to unseen people.

## What these results do not cover

- The readings on people not seen in training cover those people, not motion types not seen in training: 88% of labelled evaluation clips share a sub-dataset and motion label with training. There is one split, and the 95% CI does not include variation from split to split. People are identities as far as AMASS metadata tell, so a performer recorded in two sub-datasets without a shared name or ID can be on both sides. No reading here says which retarget generalises better.
- Auto scaling + clip grounding and the three clusters were chosen on the kinematic results over all paired clips, including the clips of the people left out of training. Those clips are therefore unseen by the tracker only. Any statement about one FRDV condition against the baseline is a selection: from four unconstrained FRDV conditions (five with constrained FRDV) in the kinematic comparison, and from three in tracking. The baseline is one retarget, with three grounding forms in tracking.
- There is one primary tracker and one primary preset: BlinkMimic2's small-dataset preset at 7,000 iterations. The library-scale preset and the second tracker give signs only, and the second tracker covers three clusters with one seed. Executed differences are level differences and include the reference-motion difference.
- The per-cluster result comes from three rule-selected clusters, one of them chosen for high FRDV self-contact, read on training clips with 3 seeds.
- Tracking is in simulation only. Self-contact is read on the link meshes, and the physical relevance of 1–2 cm overlaps is not established.
- Only clips FRDV loads are compared (85.3% of the baseline's clip list), on one robot model.
- Limb direction errors include a constant human–robot difference in body proportions; only differences between conditions are meaningful.

## Reproduce

```bash
export SMPLH_MODEL_PATH=<SMPL+H model directory>
export FRDV_MENAGERIE_REF=370c43965323c0944b433b202aac5058b5f907a9   # MuJoCo Menagerie revision, one for all conditions
uv run retarget robot=g1 dataset=amass data=<AMASS directory> out=<dir>                         # default
uv run retarget robot=g1 dataset=amass data=<AMASS directory> out=<dir> scaling=auto            # auto scaling
uv run retarget robot=g1 dataset=amass data=<AMASS directory> out=<dir> dataset.ground.mode=clip   # clip grounding
uv run retarget robot=g1 dataset=amass data=<AMASS directory> out=<dir> scaling=auto dataset.ground.mode=clip   # auto scaling + clip grounding
# constrained FRDV
uv run retarget robot=g1 dataset=amass data=<AMASS directory> out=<dir> scaling=auto dataset.ground.mode=clip \
  robot.xml=<G1 model with added capsules> robot.capsule_ground=false robot.self_collision.margin=0.0 \
  'robot.self_collision.pairs=[[torso_link,left_shoulder_yaw_link],[torso_link,right_shoulder_yaw_link],[left_hip_roll_link,left_wrist_yaw_link],[right_hip_roll_link,right_wrist_yaw_link],[left_hip_pitch_link,left_wrist_yaw_link],[right_hip_pitch_link,right_wrist_yaw_link],[pelvis,left_wrist_yaw_link],[pelvis,right_wrist_yaw_link],[left_hip_pitch_link,left_wrist_pitch_link],[right_hip_pitch_link,right_wrist_pitch_link],[torso_link,left_elbow_link],[torso_link,right_elbow_link],[torso_link,left_wrist_yaw_link],[torso_link,right_wrist_yaw_link]]'
```

- **Added capsules.** The G1 model with added capsules is the Menagerie model plus one collision capsule on each link of the 14 pairs, copied from a capsule-based G1 collision model where that model has one and otherwise (pelvis and hip-pitch links) sized from the link's collision mesh. Self-collision pairs build constraints only between capsule geometries, and the Menagerie G1 model has none, so on it the pairs build no constraint. `robot.capsule_ground=false` leaves the added capsules out of FRDV's floor constraint, which has no capsules to act on in the Menagerie model. With no pairs requested, the G1 model with added capsules reproduces the auto scaling + clip grounding output to 7e-12 (largest difference in the joint-position vector) on 212 clips.
- **Kinematic readings.** Both retargets go through forward kinematics of the same Menagerie model; the human mesh is skinned from SMPL+H; the readings are as defined above. The code for the readings is not part of this repository.
- **Tracking.** Training uses BlinkMimic2's training module: `python -m blinkmimic2.train --seed S --bones_list <clip list> --bones_lib <reference motions at 50 Hz>` followed by `${PRESET[baseline]} --no-pi_ref_skip`. The clip list is one cluster's clips (S = 0, 1, 2), all paired clips of at least 1 s, or those clips without the clips of the 85 people (S = 0–4 for the main tracking comparison, 0–2 otherwise). Trials use BlinkMimic2's rollout tool with 5 trials per clip from the first frame, no noise and the preset's terminations. Policies trained without the 85 people are rolled out from a copy of their training settings whose clip list is the full list; that is the only argument that changes. The library-scale preset is `${PRESET[main]}` with BlinkMimic2's own settings: 6,500 iterations, and the policy is the average of checkpoints 4,500–6,500. SuperMimic policies are trained with SuperMimic's own settings on the per-cluster reference motions, with 5 trials per evaluation clip.
